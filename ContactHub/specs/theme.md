# Theme

The app uses the aityx dark palette, from the aityx website
([theme.dart](../contacthub_app/lib/theme.dart)), the same as Mortgage
Hub's: the page is near-black with a violet tint (`#17161A`), panels and
cards step up through `#201E24` and `#2A272E`, text is light gray
(`#EFF2F4`), muted text `#A9ACB6`, and the primary color is the orange
accent (`#E36742`), secondary green (`#4CC38A`) and tertiary amber
(`#E8B04B`). App bars are flat and the page's color, with the title bold
and on the left. The web manifest's background and theme color are
`#17161A`.

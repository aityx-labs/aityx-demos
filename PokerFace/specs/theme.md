# Theme

The app uses the aityx dark palette, from the aityx website
([theme.dart](../pokerface_app/lib/theme.dart)), the same as Mortgage
Hub's and Contact Hub's: the page is near-black with a violet tint
(`#17161A`), panels and cards step up through `#201E24` and `#2A272E`,
text is light gray (`#EFF2F4`), muted text `#A9ACB6`, and the primary
color is the orange accent (`#E36742`), secondary green (`#4CC38A`) and
tertiary amber (`#E8B04B`). App bars are flat and the page's color, with
the title bold and on the left. The web manifest's background and theme
color are `#17161A`.

The table adds a felt, a radial green (`#2E6B4F` to `#1E4A37`) with a
wooden rail (`#4A3A2A`), and the cards are off-white (`#FAF7F2`) with red
(`#D63B3B`) or near-black suits. Stacks, bets and the pot are amber; the
player to act is ringed in orange, the winners in amber; an all-in is
red.

# Music

The app's background music: five minimal lo-fi jazz tracks, generated by
[scripts/jazz.dart](../../../scripts/jazz.dart) (`dart run scripts/jazz.dart`
from `PokerTour/`). Every sound is synthesized by the script, a mellow FM
electric piano, a sub bass, nylon guitar, vibraphone or high keys for the
motif, boom-bap drums of sines and filtered noise, tape wobble and vinyl
crackle, from notes it composes with a seeded random generator: no samples, no
recordings and no third-party music.

1. **Felt Room, 2 A.M.**: D major, 72 BPM, Dmaj9 · Bm9 · Em9 · A13sus A13, guitar
2. **Slow Fold**: A♭ major, 76 BPM, Fm9 · B♭m9 · E♭13sus E♭13 · A♭maj9, keys
3. **Pocket Kings**: E♭ major, 80 BPM, E♭maj9 · A♭maj7♯11 · Gm7 C7♭9 · Fm9 B♭13sus, vibraphone
4. **Rain on the River**: A minor, 70 BPM, Am9 · Dm9 · Fmaj7♯11 · Bm7♭5 E7♭9, guitar
5. **Short Stack Sunday**: F major, 84 BPM, B♭maj9 · Am7 D7♭9 · Gm9 · C13sus C13, vibraphone

# Music

The app's background music: five soft lounge-trio jazz tracks, generated by
[scripts/jazz.dart](../../../scripts/jazz.dart) (`dart run scripts/jazz.dart`
from `PokerTour/`). Every sound is synthesized by the script, an FM electric
piano, an additive upright bass, vibraphone, soft sax or flute, and brushes
and cymbals made of filtered noise, from notes it composes with a seeded
random generator: no samples, no recordings and no third-party music.

1. **Last Call at the Felt Room**: ballad, D♭ major, 74 BPM
2. **Pocket Kings**: medium swing on ii–V–I changes, F major, 112 BPM
3. **Rio on the River**: bossa nova, A minor, 120 BPM
4. **Slow Roll Blues**: minor blues, C minor, 84 BPM
5. **Waltz for the Short Stack**: jazz waltz, E♭ major, 100 BPM (3/4)

Privacy Policy

Poker Tour, a demo of aityx by Aletyx, Inc. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Poker Tour is a poker tournament for strategies: players write poker strategies, play them at a table in the browser, and enter them in a leaderboard. This policy says what Poker Tour collects, why, and what it does with it. aityx itself, the decision API Poker Tour calls, is covered by the aityx Privacy Policy.

What we collect

  • Your Google identity (email address, name and picture), from Google's sign-in. The app keeps the sign-in token in the browser tab's session storage until it expires or you sign out; the server checks it on every call that needs a player.
  • Your strategies and your alias: the strategies you enter in the tour and the name you choose for the leaderboard, stored with your email address in the tour's database.
  • The leaderboard: each strategy's results, shown over your alias (or your Google name). It names owners by a short hash, never by email.
  • In your browser only: your aityx key and its URL, and your music settings. Poker Tour's server never stores or logs them.
  • Logs: the server's operational logs (requests, errors), kept for 14 days. They do not hold email addresses or keys.

Anyone can look at the leaderboard without signing in. Signing in with Google is needed only to play and to enter strategies.

How we use it

To sign you in, to play the tournaments, to show the leaderboard, and to keep the service running and secure. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and Poker Tour sets no analytics or tracking cookies.

Who else sees it

  • Google signs you in, under Google's privacy policy.
  • aityx: when you play, your browser sends the game's state and your strategy to aityx with your own key.
  • Amazon Web Services hosts Poker Tour and its data in the United States.
  • Other players see your alias and your strategies' results on the leaderboard.

Keeping and deleting it

Your strategies and alias stay while the tour runs. To have them, or anything else about you, removed, or to ask what we hold, write to legal@aletyx.com. Signing out ends your session; clearing the site's data in your browser removes your key from it.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above says when it last changed.

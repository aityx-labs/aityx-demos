# Art

[reference/](reference/) holds the concept art of the five bots, the
characters of tatyx (copied from the mascot folder on 2026-11-07; the
originals' names in parentheses). Reference only: the app's own images
are made from these and live in
[tatyx_app/public/bots/](../tatyx_app/public/bots/), the orange
originals and, under one folder per team, the same five painted in the
team's colour by [tool/recolor.mjs](../tatyx_app/tool/recolor.mjs).

The look, for anything drawn new: ivory shells with orange panels and
dark joints, two glowing orange bars for eyes, soft studio light. The
hero shots are 1254 × 1254 with a transparent background; the lineup is
1536 × 1024 on an ivory floor. In the game the orange is the computer's;
a team's bots wear the team's colour on those same panels and eyes.

The names above are the concept art's (Scout, Builder, Analyzer, Monitor,
Connector); the game names the same five Anchor, Builder, Tester,
Combinator and Explorer ([The bots](../specs/characters.md)), and the
portraits the app serves are named after the game's parts.

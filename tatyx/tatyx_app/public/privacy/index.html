Privacy Policy

tatyx, a demo of aityx by Aletyx, Inc. Last updated November 8, 2026.

tatyx is a strategy game whose robots are driven by aityx: players write strategies, play them in the browser, and enter them in a leaderboard. This policy says what tatyx collects, why, and what it does with it. aityx itself, the decision API tatyx calls, is covered by the aityx Privacy Policy.

What we collect

  • Your Google identity (email address, name and picture), from Google's sign-in. The server checks Google's token once and then keeps you signed in with a session cookie (tatyx_session) for up to 30 days after your last visit; the page never sees the token again.
  • Your strategies and your alias: the strategies you enter in the tour and the name you choose for the leaderboard, stored with your email address.
  • The leaderboard: each strategy's results, shown over your alias, never your email.
  • In your browser only: your aityx key and its URL. They pass through tatyx's relay to aityx on each call and are never logged or stored by tatyx.
  • Logs: the server's operational logs (requests, errors), kept for 14 days. They do not hold email addresses or keys.

Anyone can look at the leaderboard without signing in. Signing in with Google is needed only to play and to enter strategies.

How we use it

To sign you in, to play the tournaments, to show the leaderboard, and to keep the service running and secure. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and tatyx sets no analytics or tracking cookies.

Who else sees it

  • Google signs you in, under Google's privacy policy.
  • aityx: when you play, your browser sends the game's state and your strategy to aityx with your own key.
  • Amazon Web Services hosts tatyx and its data in the United States.
  • Other players see your alias and your strategies' results on the leaderboard.

Keeping and deleting it

Your strategies and alias stay while the tour runs. To have them, or anything else about you, removed, or to ask what we hold, write to legal@aletyx.com. Signing out ends your session; clearing the site's data in your browser removes your key from it.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above says when it last changed.
